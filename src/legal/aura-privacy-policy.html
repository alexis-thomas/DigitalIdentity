Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026.

This policy covers the Aura app ("Aura") for iPhone and other devices. Aura is made by Alexis Thomas and published by Prismo Labs Ltd, Unit 13 Freeland Park, Wareham Road, Lytchett Matravers, Poole BH16 6FA, United Kingdom ("we"). For anything about your privacy, write to contact@alexisthomas.fr.

The short version: your pages live on your phone. There is no account. We never sell anything about you, and nothing you write is ever used for advertising or to train AI.


What stays on your phone

Everything you put in Aura is stored only on your device: your pages (moods, feeling words, the things you tag and what you write), photos and voice notes, stickers, what the cats remember, letters, worries, reminders and settings. If you turn it on, voice notes are turned into words on the phone itself.

  • Backups you make are encrypted with a password only you know (XChaCha20-Poly1305, with a key derived using scrypt). We can't open them.
  • Your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup, or Android's Google backup) includes Aura's notebook, as it does for other apps. That is between you and Apple or Google.
  • Erasing the notebook in Settings, or deleting the app, removes it from your device.

Aura's notes and the margin (optional)

With "Notes from Aura" turned on (Settings → Privacy; on by default), when a note, a question, a weekly letter or a reply in the margin is written for you, we send that page or line (its words, mood and tags, and the moods of your last few check-ins, never their words) to our server, which asks Anthropic's Claude to write the reply and sends it back. The text is not stored, not logged and not used to train any model, by us or by Anthropic. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, which do not allow training on it, and keeps it only briefly for safety and abuse monitoring. With "Notes from Aura" off, nothing you write leaves your phone.

The same text is checked for signs that you might be at risk, so Aura can show helplines. On the phone this uses simple word patterns; with "Notes from Aura" on, the model checks it too. Only the result is used, and nothing is kept.

Our server runs on Amazon Web Services in the United States. The only thing it keeps is, for each install, a random identifier and how many of Aura Plus's free tries it has used (four numbers), deleted a year after the last one.


Aura Plus (purchases)

Purchases are made through the App Store (or Google Play). Apple or Google handle payment; we never see your card or account details. RevenueCat keeps track of whether your install has Aura Plus: it receives the store's receipts, a random install identifier, your device's vendor identifier, and this install's Google Analytics identifier, so it can report renewals and refunds to Google Analytics (below).


Analytics and crash reports (you can turn these off)

With "Help improve Aura" turned on (Settings → Privacy; on by default), we learn how Aura is used, never what is in it:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase receives anonymous usage events: which screens are opened; that a check-in was made (whether it had words, how many photos, whether it had a voice note, never the mood); whether a note was written online or offline; when the Aura Plus page is opened and from where; plans chosen and purchases; and campaign links you arrived from. It uses an app-instance identifier, not your name or advertising identifier, and Google's advertising features are turned off.
  • Sentry receives crash reports: the error, where in the code it happened, the app version and the phone model. No screenshots, no content and no user identity.

Turn the switch off and neither is sent.

Aura has no ads and no advertising SDK. It does not use your device's advertising identifier and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so it never asks for permission to.


Apple Health (optional, iPhone)

If you turn it on, each check-in's mood and feeling words are saved to Apple Health as a "State of Mind" (never what you wrote). If you turn on "Bring in sleep and steps", those are read from Apple Health or Health Connect and kept on your phone for Patterns; they are never sent to us or to anyone else.


Legal bases, partners and transfers
  • Legal bases (UK GDPR and EU GDPR): providing the app and its notes (contract); analytics, crash reports and checking Aura Plus (our legitimate interest in a working, improving app; you can object by turning them off).
  • Processors: Anthropic (AI notes), Amazon Web Services (our server), RevenueCat (purchases), Google (Analytics for Firebase) and Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry, crash reports). Several are in the United States; transfers rely on the UK and EU standard contractual clauses or the Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep things

Your notebook is kept on your phone for as long as you keep it. Our server keeps no journal text. Analytics events are kept for the period set in Google Analytics (currently 14 months), and crash reports for Sentry's retention period (up to 90 days).


Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, object to and port your data. Most of it is already in your hands: Settings → Your pages → "Export my words", or "Tear out every page". For anything our partners hold about your install, write to us and we will take care of it. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or to your local data protection authority.


Children

Aura is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly process their data. If you believe a child has used Aura, write to us.


If you still use Aura 1.x

Versions of Aura from 2024 (1.0 to 1.0.2) worked differently: they could show ads through Google AdMob (which may use your device's advertising identifier, with your permission), and used Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Sentry for usage and crash data, and RevenueCat for purchases. Their mood entries were stored on your phone. Updating to Aura 2.0 removes the ads; your old moods come across to the new notebook on your phone.


Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and mention it in the app's release notes.
